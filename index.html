Section 01 — Digital Audit
What Gorkhas
Is Missing Right Now
⬤ CRITICAL

Inactive Website

No official hub means no merch sales, no fan registration, no SEO, and no credibility with sponsors. 6 out of 8 NPL teams have active websites. Gorkhas and Lumbini are the only two without.

⬤ CRITICAL

No YouTube Strategy

Nepal has 8.2 million YouTube users — the #2 platform in the country. The channel exists but functions as a passive video dump with no SEO, no playlists, no upload cadence, and no subscriber growth plan.

⬤ HIGH

10 Months of Silence

NPL runs Nov–Dec. For the remaining 10 months, social media activity drops to near zero. Fans who join during the season have no reason to stay engaged, so you rebuild from scratch every year.

⬤ HIGH

Instagram Behind Competitors

Chitwan Rhinos have 14K+ Instagram followers vs Gorkhas' 11K — despite Gorkhas representing Nepal's capital and being backed by Kantipur Publications, one of Nepal's biggest media houses.

⬤ HIGH

Zero Merch & E-Commerce

58,000+ Facebook fans and no jersey store. No limited edition drops. No online merchandise funnel at all. This is direct revenue left uncollected every single day.

⬤ MEDIUM

Duplicate Account Confusion

An old @kathmandugurkhas account (1,232 followers) still exists alongside the main @ktmgorkhas. This splits brand traffic, confuses fans, and dilutes search presence.

⬤ MEDIUM

Karan KC Underutilised

The marquee player has a built-in fanbase but is not being used as a content engine. No dedicated player content series, no personal brand integration, no crossover fan capture.

⬤ MEDIUM

Kantipur Media Advantage Unused

Gorkhas is owned by Kantipur Publications — the same group that runs The Kathmandu Post, Kantipur TV, and radio. This cross-promotion infrastructure is almost entirely untapped.
